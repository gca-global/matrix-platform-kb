# ADR-034: Opportunity as a stored CDL super-resource with a calculated (never materialized) pipeline stage

**Status**: Accepted
**Date**: 2026-06-17
**Deciders**: Pipeline team (matrix-pipeline 2.0) + platform team (CDL owners)
**Related**: [ADR-029](ADR-029.md) (stage-as-projection, deal-won vs settled — amended here), [ADR-025](ADR-025.md) (`referral`/`document` project-flavour resources), [ADR-033](ADR-033.md) (`showing_participation` project-flavour resource), [ADR-026](ADR-026.md) (event-sourced transaction model), [ADR-016](ADR-016.md) (`cdl-write` dispatcher), [ADR-012](ADR-012.md)/[ADR-013](ADR-013.md)/[ADR-014](ADR-014.md) (CDL split, no app service-role key), [platform-extensions.md](../../data-models/platform-extensions.md), [cdl-schema.md](../../data-models/cdl-schema.md), [opportunity-model.md](../../data-models/opportunity-model.md)

## Context

matrix-pipeline's CRM funnel has, until now, been a **pure projection over
`(Contacts × SavedSearch)`** pairs plus the related `Prospecting` /
`ContactListings` / Showing chain / `TransactionManagement` / target
`Property.StandardStatus` (see [overview.md#pipeline](../../product-specs/matrix-pipeline/wiki/overview.md#pipeline),
[ADR-029](ADR-029.md)). The KB explicitly stated there is **no stored
`Opportunity` entity** ([entities.md](../../product-specs/matrix-pipeline/wiki/entities.md),
"`Opportunity` → projection") and the **Pipeline gate**
([architecture.md](../../product-specs/matrix-pipeline/wiki/architecture.md))
forbids materializing the pipeline as a table.

Product direction has changed: sales management needs the **Opportunity** to be a
**first-class, explicit subject** — the "super-resource" that aggregates every
CRM sub-resource bound to one client intent (contact, saved search, contact
listings, showings/appointments, caravans, referrals, and the offer
`TransactionManagement`) and becomes the unit of the pipeline. An Opportunity must
be creatable from a contact *or* without a contract (e.g. originated from another
site), and it must move through **qualification → matching → viewing → contract →
closed** as the combined lifecycle of its linked sub-resources advances.

Two grounding facts informed the design:
- A `(Contacts × SavedSearch)` projection has **no stable identity** to attach
  notes, ownership, or cross-resource links to — every consumer re-derives the
  pairing. A real deal spans more than one saved search and sometimes none.
- `transaction_management` is effectively **vestigial today**: live CDL holds 2
  rows (both `Purchase Offer`, test residue), only 3/79 `history_transactional`
  rows reference a transaction key, and `TransactionManagement` does not even
  appear among emitted history `resource_name`s — Property / Contacts /
  SavedSearch drive the lifecycle, not TM.

## Decision

Introduce a **stored `opportunity` super-resource** (plus an `opportunity_link`
join table) in the **CDL** and make it the subject of the pipeline, while keeping
the **stage itself calculated and never stored**.

### D1 — `opportunity` + `opportunity_link` are stored project-flavour CDL resources

They live in the **CDL** (`ofzcokolkeejgqfjaszq`, owned by
`matrix-platform-foundation/supabase/cdl/`), co-located with the RESO resources
they aggregate. RESO DD 2.0 models no Opportunity/Deal resource, so — exactly like
`referral`, `document`, and `showing_participation` ([ADR-025](ADR-025.md),
[ADR-033](ADR-033.md)) — these are **project-flavour CDL resources** with plain
canonical snake_case columns (no `x_` prefix), governed by this ADR. They follow
the established source envelope: surrogate `id uuid` PK; natural key
`unique(source_id, source_<resource>_key)`; `is_visible`/`is_deleted`/`deleted_at`
soft-delete; `locked_fields`/`raw` jsonb; full `originating_system_*` /
`source_system_*` provenance; `created_at`/`updated_at` + `cdl_set_updated_at`
trigger.

### D2 — Stage is CALCULATED ONLY (the Pipeline gate is preserved, not broken)

The `opportunity` row stores the **anchor + linkage + minimal header only**. It
carries **no `stage` column**. The 5-stage funnel position is derived on read by a
pure function `deriveOpportunityStage()` from the linked sub-resources'
lifecycles (saved_search/prospecting, contact_listings sent, showings,
transaction_management + `Property.StandardStatus`), reusing the canonical rules
of [ADR-029](ADR-029.md) / `pipelineProjection`. Because the stage is never
materialized it **cannot drift** from canonical state — the
[architecture.md](../../product-specs/matrix-pipeline/wiki/architecture.md)
"Pipeline gate" ("stage is never a stored field / `pipeline_stages` table is
forbidden") therefore **still holds**. What changes is only the *subject* of the
projection: it shifts from the implicit `(Contacts × SavedSearch)` pair to the
explicit stored `opportunity`. ADR-029's deal-won (`PurchaseContractDate`) vs
settled (`StandardStatus = Closed`) distinction is preserved as a sub-state of the
calculated `closed` stage.

### D3 — Linkage is loose `(resource_name, resource_key)` refs, NOT hard FKs

`opportunity_link` records `(opportunity_key, resource_name, resource_key, role)`.
Cross-resource references are **loose text keys with no DB foreign-key
constraint**, consistent with every other CDL table (`*_key` witness columns are
not FKs because the source envelope keys off `(source_id, source_key)`, feeds can
arrive out of order, and the canonical `*_key` columns are not unique PKs). This
deliberately refines the implementation plan's "real FK" wording: co-location in
one database gives logical integrity + fast indexed joins, but hard FK constraints
are **not** how the CDL envelope works and would break out-of-order ingest and the
generic `cdl-write` dispatcher. Recorded as an accepted deviation.

### D4 — `transaction_management` is kept but demoted to a sub-resource

The RESO `TransactionManagement` header is **retained** (it is the only canonical
home for offer identity / syndication / Atlas interop) but is no longer authored
standalone: an offer is created in the context of an Opportunity's `contract`
stage and linked via `opportunity_link (resource_name = 'TransactionManagement')`.
The standalone "Transaction records" CRUD section on `/transactions` is
retired/relocated; the Lifecycle / Deal P&L / Country-rules tabs (the real state
machine + economics) stay. No data migration is required — existing TM rows remain
valid and simply become reachable through the link.

### D5 — Writes via `cdl-write`, reads via `cdl-read`; no app service-role key

Both resources are registered in the `cdl-write` dispatcher (insert / update /
upsert / delete with auto-stamped envelope + `HistoryTransactional` emission) and
in `cdl-read` (filterable). The app holds **no CDL service-role key**
([ADR-012](ADR-012.md)); all mutations go through the governed EFs. There is **no
stage-write path and no stage-event table** — there is no stored stage to write or
audit; transitions are observable on each sub-resource's own
`history_transactional`.

## Consequences

### Positive
- The Opportunity gains a **stable identity** to own links, ownership, notes,
  origin, and reporting — the pipeline subject is now explicit instead of
  re-derived per consumer.
- The **Pipeline gate is preserved**: no stored stage, no `pipeline_stages` table;
  stage stays a pure projection that cannot drift.
- No new precedent: reuses the `referral`/`document`/`showing_participation`
  project-flavour pattern, the `cdl-write`/`cdl-read` EFs, and the ADR-029 stage
  rules — no new EF, no new auth surface.
- An Opportunity can exist with **zero or many** saved searches / listings / offers
  (create-from-contact and create-without-contract), which the rigid
  `(Contacts × SavedSearch)` unit could not represent.

### Negative / notes
- `opportunity_link.resource_key` is a loose witness (no FK) — orphaning is
  possible if a sub-resource is hard-deleted; acceptable for a CRM aggregation
  breadcrumb (same posture as ADR-033) and surfaced as "link points at a missing
  row" in the UI rather than enforced by the DB.
- Two subjects coexist during transition: the legacy `(Contacts × SavedSearch)`
  projection (`usePipeline`) is re-based onto the opportunity; the stage rules are
  consolidated into `opportunityStage.ts` and the old `pipelineProjection.ts` rules
  are folded in.
- Creating opportunities is a manual/explicit act; contacts without an opportunity
  do not appear on the opportunity pipeline (by design — an opportunity is a
  deliberate sales intent, not every contact).

## Alternatives considered
- **Keep the pure `(Contacts × SavedSearch)` projection** (status quo, ADR-029):
  rejected — no stable identity, cannot model no-/multi-search deals, no place to
  hang ownership/links/origin.
- **Store the stage on the opportunity row** (stage as a column): rejected by
  explicit product decision — it would re-introduce drift between the stored stage
  and canonical sub-resource state and violate the Pipeline gate. Stage stays
  calculated only.
- **App-private opportunity table (Pipeline app DB)**: rejected — the opportunity
  is tightly coupled to CDL RESO resources (contact/listing/showing keys); putting
  it in the app DB would split the join across two databases and lose co-located
  indexed reads. It belongs in the CDL.
- **Real FK constraints on `opportunity_link`**: rejected — incompatible with the
  CDL source-envelope keying and out-of-order ingest (see D3).
