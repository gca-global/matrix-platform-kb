# ADR-016: Canonical-into-CDL acceleration for matrix-pipeline 2.0

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Deciders:** platform team (CDL owners) + Pipeline team (matrix-pipeline 2.0)
**Related:** ADR-008 (5-level scope), ADR-011 (ES256), ADR-012 (CDL Third-Party Auth), ADR-013 (matrix-platform-foundation owns CDL + SSO), ADR-014 (Unified MLS ingestion pipeline), ADR-015 (CDL Pipeline EF surface — Proposed)
**Supersedes:** —
**Superseded by:** —

## Context

[ADR-015](ADR-015.md) catalogued the CDL gap for the `matrix-pipeline` 2.0 CRM and
proposed six platform deliverables (KB drift fix, per-resource read/write EFs, a
roster decision, and a Phase-2+ migration roadmap with ETAs). Rather than stage
those over several quarters, the decision here is to **accelerate the entire
canonical-into-CDL move into one release** so the Lovable CRM build (phases.md)
targets CDL from day one instead of queuing every write in app-DB `*_pending`
tables.

A fresh live CDL audit (`user-supabase-cdl` MCP, 2026-05-29) confirmed the
ADR-015 snapshot and added one execution finding (see Decision §4).

State at audit time:
- **12 of ~25** canonical tables present and RESO-compliant.
- **2 present but non-canonical** with **RLS disabled** — `contact_listings`
  (24,979 rows) + `contact_listing_notes` — an active CDL access-gate violation.
- **9 missing entirely** — `saved_search`, `prospecting`, `showing_availability`,
  `showing_request`, `showing` (recorded fact), `lock_or_box`, `caravan`,
  `caravan_stop`, `transaction_management`.
- **Zero broker-scope write surface** into CDL.

## Decision

### #1 — Land the 9 missing canonical tables now

Migration `20260529160000_pipeline_canonical_new_tables.sql` creates all 9 tables
on the Phase-1 hybrid pattern (`id uuid` PK, `unique(source_id, source_*_key)`,
`content_hash`, soft-delete, RESO snake_case typed columns from
`reso-dd-kb/wiki/agent-docs/resources/*.md`, `raw jsonb` GIN-indexed,
`locked_fields jsonb`, `updated_at` trigger). Cross-resource refs are loose `text`
`*_key` columns (no hard FK — feeds sync out of order).

> **Naming:** `public.showings` (Phase-1) = RESO **ShowingAppointment**;
> `public.showing` (Phase-2) = RESO **Showing** (recorded fact). Two distinct RESO
> resources; do not conflate. This resolves the ADR-015 naming-drift item.

### #2 — Re-model `contact_listings` + `contact_listing_notes` and adopt into the repo

Migration `20260529161000_pipeline_contact_listings_remodel.sql` adds the canonical
RESO envelope + engagement columns (additive), backfills canonical keys, migrates
inline `notes` text into child `contact_listing_notes` rows, and brings both
tables — previously **live-only** — under the foundation repo. Closes ADR-015 #4's
schema half.

### #3 — Transaction-management split (canonical vs economics)

`public.transaction_management` carries **only the 4 canonical RESO fields**
(`transaction_key`, `transaction_id`, `transaction_type`, `modification_timestamp`).
Deal **offer amount, commission, and per-deal P&L** have **no RESO resource** and
stay **app-private** in the CRM app DB (Commission Engine). This is an explicit
**escape-hatch** deviation (RESO has no deal-economics resource) — recorded here
and in `wiki/architecture.md#escape-hatch`.

### #4 — RLS now; Pattern B as the tracked target

All 9 new + 2 re-modeled tables enable RLS:
- Reference / low-sensitivity (`saved_search`, `showing_availability`,
  `showing_request`, `showing`, `caravan`, `caravan_stop`,
  `transaction_management`): `service_role` full DML + `authenticated` SELECT
  (`is_deleted = false`).
- PII / engagement / access-audit (`prospecting`, `lock_or_box`,
  `contact_listings`, `contact_listing_notes`): **`service_role`-only** (EF-gated;
  anon/authenticated access revoked) — mirrors `contacts`.

This closes the 2 critical engagement-table gate violations. Full scope-aware
**Pattern B** (SSO-JWT `scope`/`crud` policies per `SECURITY_MODEL.md`) remains an
S1 follow-up gated to roadmap milestone `O-CLIENT-PRIVACY`.

> **Execution finding (supersedes the ADR-015 / plan tentative mapping):** the live
> `contact_listings.relationship` column holds **listing-side provenance**
> (`Seller` 15,865 / `Developer` 9,114), **NOT** the canonical buyer-engagement
> `ContactListingPreference` (`Favorite`/`Possibility`/`Discard`). They are
> semantically different. The re-model therefore **retains** the provenance columns
> (`relationship`, `contact_id`, `property_id`, `resource_name`, `class_name`,
> `notes`) and adds the canonical engagement columns **nullable** for forward CRM
> use. **No `relationship → contact_listing_preference` mapping is applied.** The
> legacy `contact_id` / `property_id` columns are kept through a soak period and
> dropped only after backfill verification.

### #5 — One `cdl-write` dispatcher instead of per-resource write EFs

ADR-015 #6 proposed a `cdl-<resource>-write` per resource. This ADR **supersedes
that** with a single generic dispatcher `cdl-write` (`{ resource, op, record/key }`,
service-role inside the EF, SSO-JWT broker scope, emits `HistoryTransactional` on
every mutating op). Rationale: the scope/tenant/audit contract is uniform across
resources, so one bounded dispatcher ships faster and is cheaper to maintain than
14 near-identical EFs. The PII/engagement read paths remain dedicated EFs
(`cdl-contacts-read`, `cdl-contact-listings-read`) because their redaction shape
differs. This folds ADR-015 #2 (`cdl-contacts-write`), #3 (`cdl-history-emit`),
and #4 (`cdl-contact-listings-write`) into `cdl-write`.

### #6 — ADR-015 #1 (KB drift) resolved here

`cdl-schema.md` is patched in the same change: the Teams DROP (PR1.5) is reflected,
`showing_appointments` → `showings` naming is corrected, and the Phase-2 expansion
(9 new tables + contact-listings re-model) is documented. ADR-015 #1 is **closed**.

### #7 — Roster (ADR-015 #5) NOT closed here

Teams / TeamMembers / OUID stay out of scope. Tracked as roadmap milestone
`O-ROSTER` (`blocked`, gated on ADR-015 #5). Pipeline favours Option B (derive team
identity from SSO groups).

## Consequences

**For Pipeline:**
- Phases 1–4 write canonical RESO resources directly to CDL via `cdl-write` from
  day one; `*_pending` queues remain only as the `EF_NOT_AVAILABLE` fallback.
- The READ-C placeholders (`cdl-contacts-read`, `cdl-contact-listings-read`) are
  now live; no more `501` pending banners for those.
- Compliance gate #3 (Audit) is satisfiable from broker UI — every `cdl-write` op
  emits a `HistoryTransactional` row.

**For Platform:**
- One write EF surface (`cdl-write`) to maintain instead of a per-resource family.
- KB stops drifting from live CDL.
- Pattern B rollout on the 4 critical tables is the only remaining engagement-data
  security task (S1, `O-CLIENT-PRIVACY`).

**For other apps (Atlas, future):**
- `mls-sync` stays admin-only (unchanged). `cdl-write` is broker-scope and
  general-purpose; any app with broker scope can use it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Stage the migration over quarters per ADR-015 #6** — rejected; queuing every
  Phase-1–4 write in app-DB `*_pending` and reconciling later is more total work
  and delays canonical-home benefits (multi-app reads, audit).
- **Per-resource write EFs (`cdl-<resource>-write`)** — rejected in favour of the
  `cdl-write` dispatcher (uniform contract; far less code).
- **Map `relationship` → `contact_listing_preference`** — rejected after the live
  audit proved different semantics (provenance vs buyer preference).
- **Type `transaction_management` economics into CDL** — rejected; RESO has no
  deal-economics resource. Economics stay app-private (escape hatch).
